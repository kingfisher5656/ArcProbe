# Official potential badge artwork

Retrieved 2026-10-11 from the public Arcaea Online frontend. Artwork belongs to lowiro.

These are the same transparent rank backgrounds used by the official friends page. They include the rings and stars; ArcProbe draws its own number over them.

Source frontend: https://arcaea.lowiro.com/assets/index-gOl1WKik.js

The frontend maps friend.icon to https://webassets.lowiro.com/chr/{icon}.png. Main-account character_stats contains the equipped character’s icon. Portraits are downloaded on demand and are not bundled.
